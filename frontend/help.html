Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I create a user? Go to Users → Add User, complete the form, and select Add User.
  • How do I create a task? Go to TODOs → Add New TODO, choose a user, then enter the task details.
  • How do I return to the dashboard? Click Dashboard in the sidebar navigation.
  • Where do I update my settings? Select Settings from the sidebar menu, then update your Profile or Preferences.
  • How do I find a record? Use the search field on the Users or TODOs page and move through the results with pagination.
