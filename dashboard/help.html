Help & Support

Learn how to use TaskFlow.

How do I manage users?

Open the Users page from the sidebar to view and manage registered users.

How do I create a TODO?

Open the TODOs page, enter your task, and click Add TODO.

How do I complete a TODO?

Click the Complete button on a TODO card.

How do I logout?

Click Logout on the sidebar.
